## AI Coaching — A Specific Weekly Offering

### Stop Experimenting With AI. Start Mastering It.

Within the broader coaching umbrella, I run a **structured weekly program** focused specifically on building real AI fluency. This isn't dabbling with prompts — it's a systematic approach to mastering AI tools and making them a genuine part of how you work.

**What we cover:**
- **AI Tools Mastery** — ChatGPT, Claude, and choosing the right tool for the right job with precision
- **Prompting Strategy** — Build prompting systems tailored to your stack, your clients, your goals
- **AI-Native Development** — Think about AI as a first-class component in how you design, build, and ship
- **Workflow Automation** — Identify your highest-leverage tasks and redesign them around AI

**Format:** Weekly group sessions — learn alongside other developers and founders who are serious about going deeper with AI.

**Who it's for:** Developers and founders ready to go beyond dabbling. If you've played with AI tools but haven't built a real system around them, this is where you start.
